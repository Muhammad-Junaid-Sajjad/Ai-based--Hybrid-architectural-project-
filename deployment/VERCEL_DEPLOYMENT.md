# 🚀 Vercel Deployment Guide

**Deploy your website online in 5 minutes**

---

## Step 1: Sign Up

1. Go to https://vercel.com/signup
2. Click "Continue with GitHub"
3. Authorize Vercel

---

## Step 2: Import Project

1. Click "New Project"
2. Import `hybrid-cpu-architecture` repository
3. Configure:
   - Framework: Other
   - Root Directory: `./`
   - Build Command: (leave empty)
   - Output Directory: `frontend`
   - Install Command: (leave empty)
4. Click "Deploy"

---

## Step 3: Get Your URL

After 1-2 minutes:
- Your site is live!
- URL: `https://hybrid-cpu-architecture.vercel.app`

---

## Step 4: Update Links

In `frontend/index.html`, replace:
- `YOUR_USERNAME` → your GitHub username
- Add your Vercel URL

Commit and push → Vercel auto-redeploys!

---

**Deployment Complete!** ✅

**Your site**: https://hybrid-cpu-architecture.vercel.app
